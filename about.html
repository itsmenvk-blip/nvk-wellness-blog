About NVK Wellness Blog

NVK Wellness Blog explores wellness as a whole-life concept.

We believe wellbeing includes more than physical health. It also includes financial stability, happiness, meaningful relationships, self-respect, emotional balance, spirituality, personal growth and a sense of purpose.

The site shares ideas, observations, learning and perspectives intended to help readers think, learn and live more consciously. We are an independent publication. We are not a medical organization, and we do not claim professional credentials.
